# Cover Art Concepts for *What the House Keeps*

## The Warm Room

**Visual concept:** A single softly glowing window in an otherwise black, frost-rimed house, its warmth reading as wrong rather than welcoming — the one lit room in a dead facade.
**Target audience:** Literary gothic horror readers who favor atmosphere and dread over gore; fans of *The Haunting of Hill House* and Shirley Jackson.

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Vertical book cover, 1:1.6 aspect ratio, atmospheric gothic horror illustration in muted painterly digital art. A tall, decaying Victorian house in near-total silhouette against a starless slate-black night, exterior walls glazed with frost and ice. Every window is dark except one upstairs window glowing a sickly amber-orange, the warmth unnatural and unsettling rather than inviting. A faint child-sized silhouette stands motionless in the lit window. Foreground: a low, ice-skinned pond reflecting the single warm window as a smeared orange streak. Color palette: deep blacks, cold steel-blue, bone-white frost, one bruised amber light source. Mood: dread, isolation, supernatural wrongness, grief, suffocating quiet. Composition: house centered and looming, lit window as the focal point in the upper-middle third, pond and reflection occupying the lower third. Render the title text "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in large distressed serif capitals across the top quarter in frost-white with subtle ice cracking through the letterforms. Render the author name "GREG RATAJIK" in smaller clean serif capitals centered along the bottom edge in pale grey. Thumbnail-legible, high contrast.
```

### Ideogram Typography Variant

```
Vertical 1:1.6 gothic horror book cover, painterly muted style. Black frost-covered Victorian house silhouette against a starless night, a single upstairs window glowing wrong amber-orange with a small child-shaped shadow inside, an ice-skinned pond below reflecting the light. Palette: black, steel-blue, frost-white, one bruised amber glow. Bold integrated typography: title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in large weathered serif capitals across the top, frost-white letters fractured with hairline ice cracks, dominating the upper third. Author "GREG RATAJIK" in refined smaller serif capitals at the bottom in soft grey. Atmosphere of cold dread and isolation. High contrast, thumbnail-readable.
```

## The Ledger Hand

**Visual concept:** A close, candle-lit page of an old account-book where a column of handwritten names ends on a name that is clearly the reader's stand-in — the horror of an inventory that has already written your entry.
**Target audience:** Readers drawn to slow-burn literary horror, found-document and epistolary dread, and bibliophile aesthetics.

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Vertical book cover, 1:1.6 aspect ratio, photorealistic still-life in low warm candlelight. Extreme close-up of an aged, foxed, water-stained ledger page lying open on dark wood, filled with a column of antique fountain-pen handwriting: names and dates in faded sepia ink. The ink grows fresher and darker toward the bottom of the column, where the final entry is wet and gleaming. A single guttering candle just out of frame throws warm light from the upper left, deep shadow swallowing the page edges. A child's red rubber ball rests at the corner of the page, slightly out of focus. Color palette: sepia, aged ivory paper, oxblood ink, deep umber shadow, one spot of saturated red from the ball. Mood: clerical menace, dread, intimacy, the uncanny precision of a record that should not exist. Composition: ledger page filling the frame at a slight angle, the freshly-inked final line as the focal point in the lower-middle, generous dark space at top. Render the title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in elegant tall serif capitals across the top third, ink-black with a hand-lettered quality as though written by the same pen. Render "GREG RATAJIK" smaller in matching serif beneath the title. Text reads clearly at thumbnail size.
```

### Ideogram Typography Variant

```
Vertical 1:1.6 literary horror book cover, photorealistic candlelit still-life. Close-up of an aged foxed ledger page with a column of antique handwritten names and dates in fading sepia ink, the bottom entry freshly wet and dark, a single red rubber ball resting on the corner, deep umber shadows at the edges. Palette: sepia, ivory, oxblood ink, umber, one red accent. Bold integrated hand-lettered typography: title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in tall elegant serif capitals across the top in ink-black, styled to match the fountain-pen handwriting on the page. Author "GREG RATAJIK" in smaller matching serif directly below. Atmosphere of quiet clerical dread. High contrast and legible as a thumbnail.
```

## Under the Ice

**Visual concept:** Viewed from directly above, the pale shape of a child suspended just beneath a skin of dark ice — the threshold between lost and kept rendered as a single arresting image.
**Target audience:** Horror readers who respond to bold, minimal, high-impact conceptual covers; fans of literary drowning and grief horror.

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Vertical book cover, 1:1.6 aspect ratio, moody fine-art photographic composition, top-down overhead view. The dark frozen surface of a pond, thin uneven ice cracked in places, the water beneath nearly black. Just below the ice, a pale, blurred, child-sized human shape floats face-up, indistinct and dreamlike, fingers faintly raised toward the surface. Surface details: a few dead leaves frozen into the ice, hairline white fracture lines radiating outward. Color palette: near-black water, slate-grey and white ice, the cold blue-grey pallor of the submerged shape, no warm tones. Mood: drowning dread, grief, stillness, the uncanny boundary between death and capture. Composition: the submerged figure offset slightly below center as the focal point, vast dark ice filling the surrounding frame, intentional negative space at the top for text. Render the title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in stark condensed sans-serif capitals across the upper third in icy white, letters appearing to crack like the ice around them. Render the author name "GREG RATAJIK" in small spaced sans-serif capitals at the very bottom in pale frost-grey. Bold, minimal, instantly readable as a thumbnail.
```

## The Count in the Walls

**Visual concept:** A cold, peeling interior corridor where faint handwritten numbers bleed through the wallpaper, climbing toward a single circled figure — the auction count made visible.
**Target audience:** Readers who like cerebral, motif-driven horror and unusual conceptual covers that reward a second look.

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Vertical book cover, 1:1.6 aspect ratio, eerie painterly digital illustration with a faded vintage texture. A narrow, cold Victorian hallway in deep one-point perspective, peeling water-stained wallpaper, dim grey light, a closed door at the far end with a thin seam of warm amber light beneath it. Across the wallpaper, faint ghostly handwritten numerals bleed through the pattern in sepia ink, ascending in sequence and growing larger and darker as they climb toward a single number circled near the lit door at the vanishing point. Color palette: cold grey-green, faded ivory wallpaper, sepia ink, deep shadow, one thread of warm amber at the far door. Mood: creeping dread, claustrophobia, the bureaucratic horror of being counted, supernatural quiet. Composition: hallway receding to a central vanishing point as the focal point, the circled number and door at the end, wallpaper numerals leading the eye inward, dark space at the top for title. Render the title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in tall weathered serif capitals across the top third in faded bone-white. Render "GREG RATAJIK" in smaller serif capitals centered at the bottom in muted grey. Strong silhouette and contrast for thumbnail legibility.
```

## The Cataloguer's Tag

**Visual concept:** A worn auction lot-tag tied with string to an empty child's chair in a cold, dim room — the cataloguer's own tools quietly turned into the instrument of her undoing.
**Target audience:** Literary horror readers who appreciate restrained, symbolic single-object covers and the slow turn of an ordinary thing into a threat.

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Vertical book cover, 1:1.6 aspect ratio, atmospheric photorealistic still-life in cold, thin grey light. A small empty child-sized wooden chair sits alone in a bare, dim room with faded peeling wallpaper and a single frosted window. Tied to the chair with rough twine hangs a buff-colored paper auction lot-tag, on which a handwritten number is clearly visible. A faint cushion-dent remains in the chair seat as though just vacated. A single red rubber ball rests on the floorboards beside one chair leg. Cold daylight falls from a window at left; the rest of the room dissolves into shadow. Color palette: desaturated greys and dust-browns, faded wallpaper, cold window-blue, the buff tag, one muted red accent. Mood: grief, abandonment, quiet menace, the uncanny presence of an absence. Composition: chair and tag centered as the focal point in the lower-middle two-thirds, frosted window and shadow framing it, clear darker space across the top for the title. Render the title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in distressed serif capitals across the top quarter in chalky off-white. Render the author name "GREG RATAJIK" in smaller clean serif capitals beneath the title in pale grey. High contrast, clearly legible as a small thumbnail.
```
