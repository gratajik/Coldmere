# Cover Art Concepts for *What the House Keeps*

## The Warm Room

**Visual concept:** A single doorway in a frozen house glowing with impossible warmth — the only heat in a house of ice, framed as an invitation that is really a trap. The warmth reads as wrong, not welcoming.
**Target audience:** Literary gothic horror readers drawn to atmospheric dread (Shirley Jackson, *The Little Stranger*, Sarah Waters) over splatter.

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Atmospheric literary gothic horror book cover, vertical 1:1.6 aspect ratio. A dim, frost-rimed Victorian upstairs hallway rendered in cold desaturated blue-grey, walls faintly crusted with ice crystals. At the center, a single ajar wooden door spills an unnatural amber-orange warmth — too warm, too alive — casting long warped light across the cold floorboards. Inside the doorway, the faint silhouette of an empty high-backed reading chair facing a small window. Painterly digital illustration with fine detail, muted gothic palette of slate blue, charcoal, and a sickly warm ember glow. Single focal point on the glowing doorway, deep shadow surrounding it, strong vertical composition with cold negative space at top. Mood: dread, wrongness, claustrophobic stillness, the uncanny. Render the title text "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in a tall, elegant distressed serif font in cold bone-white, placed across the upper third. Render the author name "GREG RATAJIK" in a smaller matching serif at the very bottom center in muted grey. Cinematic, eerie, high contrast, thumbnail-legible.
```

## The Shape Under the Ice

**Visual concept:** A skinned-over pond at dusk with a faint child-sized shape suspended just beneath the ice. The horror is quiet and ambiguous — is it a reflection, a memory, or something kept?
**Target audience:** Readers of psychological/grief-driven horror and drowned-child gothic (*The Loney*, *Bird Box*–adjacent dread).

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Haunting literary horror book cover, vertical 1:1.6 aspect ratio. Overhead-angled view of a low, frozen mill-pond at twilight, surface skinned with thin cracked ice catching pale violet and steel-grey light. Just beneath the ice, a faint, blurred child-sized silhouette suspended in dark water, barely visible, deeply ambiguous. Bare black reeds at the pond's edge. Near the surface, a single small red rubber ball resting on the ice — the only spot of color. Photographic-painterly hybrid style, fine textured realism, cold limited palette of frozen blue, grey-violet, and black with one stark red accent. Strong central focal point on the shape under the ice, eerie negative space of empty ice surrounding it. Mood: grief, drowning dread, frozen loss, the unrecovered. Render the title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in a stark condensed serif in frost-white across the top, slightly fractured like cracking ice. Render "GREG RATAJIK" smaller in pale grey serif centered at the bottom. Atmospheric, minimal, unsettling, reads clearly as a thumbnail.
```

## The Ledger

**Visual concept:** An open antique ledger filling the frame, columns of handwritten names and dates trailing into shadow — and the last entry, freshly written, is the reader's own dread. Inventory as instrument of horror.
**Target audience:** Literary horror readers who love a slow reveal and a documentary/object-driven hook; fans of epistolary and archival dread.

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Gothic literary horror book cover, vertical 1:1.6 aspect ratio. Extreme close-up of an open antique leather-bound ledger lying on a dark wooden surface, lit by a single warm guttering oil lamp from the side. Columns of faded sepia ink handwriting — names and dates and single one-word notations — march down the foxed, water-stained pages and vanish into deep shadow at the spine. One fresh entry near the bottom stands out in darker, wetter ink. A small key and a coil of dust nearby. Photorealistic still-life with painterly chiaroscuro, rich tenebrist lighting, palette of aged parchment cream, sepia, deep brown-black, and a single warm lamp glow. Central focal point on the lower ledger page and its fresh entry, heavy shadow framing the composition top and edges. Mood: clerical dread, complicity, accumulation, the uncanny weight of records. Render the title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in an aged engraved serif in lamp-lit cream across the upper portion, integrated as if pressed into the page. Render "GREG RATAJIK" in smaller faded ink-brown serif at the bottom edge. Moody, intimate, high contrast, thumbnail-legible.
```

## The Count in the Walls

**Visual concept:** A faceless house facade at night where the windows form an ascending sequence of glowing numbers — the house itself counting toward a final lot. Bureaucratic horror made architectural.
**Target audience:** Readers drawn to concept-forward, design-led horror covers and the slow-dread "the house is the monster" subgenre.

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Conceptual literary horror book cover, vertical 1:1.6 aspect ratio. A tall, austere Victorian house facade silhouetted in flat black against a bruised slate-grey winter night sky, bare trees flanking it. The house's many dark windows glow faintly amber, and within a vertical column of them, ghostly numerals are visible — ascending numbers climbing toward the topmost window, which burns brightest of all. Thin cold fog at the base. Bold graphic illustration with minimal detail, strong negative space, restrained palette of black, deep slate blue, ash grey, and dim ember-amber. Centered symmetrical composition, single focal pull up the column of numbered windows to the bright top window. Mood: countdown, inevitability, the house as antagonist, dread. Render the title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in a tall sharp modern serif in cold white across the sky in the upper third. Render "GREG RATAJIK" smaller in pale grey beneath the house at the bottom center. Stark, iconic, design-forward, instantly legible as a thumbnail.
```

## The One Who Looked Away

**Visual concept:** A lone adult figure silhouetted on a frozen shore, back to us, facing a small distant child-shape at the water's edge across the ice. The unbridgeable distance between guilt and the lost.
**Target audience:** Literary fiction crossover readers and grief-horror fans who respond to human-scale, emotionally resonant imagery.

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Literary gothic horror book cover, vertical 1:1.6 aspect ratio. A lone adult woman in a dark coat stands in the foreground, seen from behind in full silhouette, on the frozen rough ground above a low ice-skinned pond at dusk. Far across the ice at the water's edge stands a small, still child-sized silhouette facing back toward her, tiny and ambiguous. A large dim gothic house looms upslope behind her, faintly lit in a single warm window. Bleak winter light, heavy low cloud. Painterly cinematic illustration, cold atmospheric palette of grey-blue, dusk lavender, charcoal, and a single warm window glow. Vertical composition with the woman lower-foreground and the child as distant focal counterpoint across deep empty space. Mood: grief, guilt, irreversible distance, quiet horror. Render the title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in an elegant tall serif in pale frost-white across the upper third of the sky. Render "GREG RATAJIK" in a smaller matching serif at the bottom center in soft grey. Emotional, atmospheric, high contrast, reads strongly at thumbnail size.
```
