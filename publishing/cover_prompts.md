# Cover Art Prompts: *What the House Keeps* by Greg Ratajik

---

## The Warm Room Window

**Visual concept:** A single lit window glowing blood-warm in the dark facade of a cold gothic house, the only warmth in a frozen world, with the suggestion of an empty chair facing out.
**Target audience:** Literary horror readers, fans of slow-burn haunted house fiction (Shirley Jackson, Catriona Ward).

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Vertical book cover, 1:1.6 aspect ratio, literary gothic horror. A dark Victorian house facade rendered in deep slate-blue and near-black, lit only by frost and a thin moon. One small upstairs window glows an unnatural blood-warm amber-orange, the single warm light in an entirely cold, frozen composition. Inside the window, the faint silhouette of an empty high-backed reading chair facing outward. Painterly, muted, atmospheric oil-painting style with fine grain and cold mist at the edges. Composition centered vertically, the glowing window as focal point in the upper-middle third, dark negative space below for text. Lighting: single warm interior glow against pervasive cold blue darkness, high contrast. Color palette: slate blue, charcoal black, frost white, with one isolated warm amber. Mood: dread, isolation, wrongness, quiet menace, grief. Title text "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in tall condensed serif capitals, frost-white with subtle distress, placed across the lower third. Author name "GREG RATAJIK" in smaller spaced serif capitals beneath the title, muted grey. Typography elegant and restrained, integrated cleanly into the dark lower portion. Reads clearly as a thumbnail.
```

### Ideogram Typography Variant

```
Vertical 1:1.6 gothic horror book cover. Dark slate-blue and black Victorian house facade, one upstairs window glowing blood-warm amber, the only light in a frozen scene, faint empty chair silhouette inside. Cold mist, painterly muted style. INTEGRATED TYPOGRAPHY IS THE FOCUS: title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in tall condensed distressed serif capitals, frost-white, spanning the lower third with strong presence. Author "GREG RATAJIK" in smaller spaced serif caps directly below in muted grey. Letterforms slightly cold and weathered. High contrast, thumbnail-legible. Palette: slate blue, charcoal, frost white, single amber accent. Mood: dread, isolation, quiet menace.
```

## The Ledger Hand

**Visual concept:** An overhead view of an open antique ledger, columns of names and dates in faded ink, with one final entry glowing or freshly wet — the protagonist's own name written ahead of time.
**Target audience:** Readers drawn to bureaucratic/clerical horror, mystery-tinged gothic, slow-dread literary fiction.

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Vertical book cover, 1:1.6 aspect ratio, literary gothic horror. Overhead flat-lay of an open antique ledger book on a dark wooden surface, aged cream pages with foxing and water-staining. Long handwritten columns of names and dates in faded brown-black ink, deliberately illegible except for one fresh entry near the bottom rendered in darker, wet-looking ink as if just written. A single drop of pond water beads on the page. Cold lamplight rakes across from the upper left, casting long shadows in the paper's texture. Painterly realistic style, fine archival detail, shallow depth of field. Composition: ledger fills the frame, the fresh entry as focal point in the lower-middle, room at top for title. Lighting: low warm lamplight against cold shadow, intimate and ominous. Color palette: aged cream, sepia, ink-brown, deep shadow black, a hint of cold blue. Mood: dread, inevitability, clerical horror, something counted. Title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in classic serif capitals stamped across the upper third in dark ink-brown, as if printed onto the page. Author name "GREG RATAJIK" in smaller serif capitals below the title, faded ink tone. Typography styled to echo the handwriting and aged paper. Thumbnail-legible.
```

### Ideogram Typography Variant

```
Vertical 1:1.6 gothic horror cover. Overhead antique ledger, aged cream pages with foxing, faded columns of handwritten names and dates, one fresh wet-ink entry near bottom, a bead of pond water. Cold raking lamplight. TYPOGRAPHY CENTRAL: title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in stamped classic serif capitals across the upper third, dark ink-brown as if pressed into the paper. Author "GREG RATAJIK" in smaller faded serif caps below. Letterforms aged and inky to match the handwriting. High contrast, thumbnail-clear. Palette: cream, sepia, ink-brown, shadow black, faint cold blue. Mood: inevitability, dread, the count.
```

## The Shape Under the Ice

**Visual concept:** A frozen mill-pond seen from above, skinned with thin ice, and beneath it the faint child-sized silhouette of a figure that should not be there.
**Target audience:** Atmospheric horror readers, fans of grief-driven supernatural fiction and unrecovered-body dread.

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Vertical book cover, 1:1.6 aspect ratio, literary gothic horror. A dark frozen mill-pond viewed from a high angle, surface skinned with thin translucent ice showing cracks and frost-fern patterns. Beneath the ice, faintly visible, the pale child-sized silhouette of a small figure suspended just below the surface, blurred and indistinct, facing upward. Surrounding the pond, hints of frozen reeds and cold black water at the edges. Painterly, eerie, muted realism with soft focus on the submerged shape. Composition: the pond fills the frame, the under-ice figure as focal point slightly below center, clean darker margin at top and bottom for text. Lighting: pale grey winter light from above, cold and flat, faint glow rising from beneath the ice. Color palette: dark teal, slate grey, frost white, pale bone, black water. Mood: grief, loss, the threshold between lost and kept, quiet horror. Title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in tall thin serif capitals, frost-white, placed across the top third. Author name "GREG RATAJIK" in smaller spaced capitals at the bottom edge, pale grey. Typography cold and clean, integrated over the ice. Thumbnail-legible.
```

## The Red Ball

**Visual concept:** A single bright red rubber ball resting on a cold, dim wooden floor inside an empty room — the one point of color in a desaturated, grief-haunted space.
**Target audience:** Minimalist-cover lovers, literary horror readers who respond to a single uncanny object.

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Vertical book cover, 1:1.6 aspect ratio, literary gothic horror, minimalist. A single bright red rubber ball resting alone on the dusty floorboards of a dim, empty, cold room. Everything else heavily desaturated into greys and cold browns — bare floorboards, a sliver of skirting board, dust, faint window light. The ball is the only saturated color, slightly too vivid, casting a soft shadow. Painterly realism with fine dust and grain, shallow depth of field, the ball sharp and the room falling into shadow. Composition: ball positioned in the lower-middle as focal point, vast empty dim floor and wall above for title space. Lighting: thin cold daylight from off-frame, low and grey, the ball catching a faint highlight. Color palette: desaturated greys, cold taupe, dust-beige, deep shadow, single bold scarlet. Mood: absence, lost childhood, unease, grief, wrongness. Title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in stark condensed sans-serif capitals, off-white, placed across the upper portion of the empty wall. Author name "GREG RATAJIK" in smaller spaced sans-serif capitals beneath the title, muted grey. Typography modern, clean, restrained, integrated into the dim wall space. Thumbnail-legible with the red ball drawing the eye.
```

## The House at the End of Fen Lane

**Visual concept:** A lone gothic house at the end of a long single-track lane crossing frozen fen country, dwarfed by an oncoming storm sky — isolation made total.
**Target audience:** Classic haunted-house readers, fans of landscape-driven gothic and remote-setting horror.

### Full Standalone Prompt

```
Vertical book cover, 1:1.6 aspect ratio, literary gothic horror. A long single-track lane recedes from the foreground into bleak frozen fen country, crossing a small bridge over a culvert, leading to a lone dark Victorian house at the vanishing point. A vast, low, bruised storm sky bears down — a gathering nor'easter, heavy clouds in slate and charcoal. The house is small in the frame, isolated, one tiny warm-lit window. Frost-pale fields and reeds, a thin band of cold light at the horizon. Painterly atmospheric landscape, moody and cinematic, fine mist. Composition: lane leads the eye from bottom-center to the distant house at the lower-middle horizon, enormous storm sky filling the upper two-thirds for title space. Lighting: dramatic stormlight, dark above and a sliver of pale cold light at the horizon, one warm pinprick window. Color palette: storm slate, charcoal, frost white, cold ochre horizon, single warm amber speck. Mood: isolation, foreboding, sealing-in, dread of approach. Title "WHAT THE HOUSE KEEPS" in tall condensed serif capitals, frost-white, spanning the storm sky in the upper third. Author name "GREG RATAJIK" in smaller spaced serif capitals just below the title, pale grey. Typography dramatic and clean against the clouds. Thumbnail-legible.
```

**Top 2 recommendations:** *The Warm Room Window* (strongest single-image hook, distills the premise's cold/warm inversion) and *The Ledger Hand* (most distinctive to this book's clerical-horror identity) — both include Ideogram typography variants above.
