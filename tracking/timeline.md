# Timeline



## Chapter 1

- Day 1 (Thursday), dusk into night: Iris drives the eleven miles down Fen Lane, crosses the last cattle-grid at last light, arrives at Coldmere.
- Day 1, dusk: glimpses the mill-pond from the drive, skinned with the first thin ice of the year.
- Day 1, evening: works the ground floor by failing daylight, room to room with the floor plan, and finds the downstairs count won't square — roughly one yard of depth unaccounted at the back of the house.
- Day 1, night: dark falls, windows go black; Iris writes her plan of attack, lights the oil lamp in the hall, does not go upstairs; one margin note left open and underlined.
