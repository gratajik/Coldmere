# Timeline



## Chapter 1

- Day 1, Thursday, dusk into dark: Iris arrives at Coldmere, parks on the gravel, walks the front and the ground floor, counts the rooms against the plan, finds the count short, logs it, and settles to sleep in the front room without going upstairs.

## Chapter 2
