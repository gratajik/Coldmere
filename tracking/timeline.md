# Timeline



## Chapter 1

- Day 1 (Thursday), dusk into night: Iris drives the eleven miles down Fen Lane, crosses the last cattle-grid at last light, arrives at Coldmere.
- Day 1, dusk: glimpses the mill-pond from the drive, skinned with the first thin ice of the year.
- Day 1, evening: works the ground floor by failing daylight, room to room with the floor plan, and finds the downstairs count won't square — roughly one yard of depth unaccounted at the back of the house.
- Day 1, night: dark falls, windows go black; Iris writes her plan of attack, lights the oil lamp in the hall, does not go upstairs; one margin note left open and underlined.

## Chapter 2

- Day 1 (Thursday), evening: Iris settles into the kitchen for the night, eats her own provisions, puts on a wool layer against the cold; the wind rises at the eaves.
- Day 1, evening: Iris confirms no mobile signal; carries the lamp to the hall and tests the landline — the dial tone arrives slow (a held half-second of quiet first).
- Day 1, evening: Iris dials the office; it rings twelve times unanswered (after-hours); she decides to try again in the morning.
- Day 1, night: Iris beds down in the kitchen chair, lamp turned low; sleeps poorly; near the bottom of sleep hears/dreams the open-line tone going on in the cold hall.
